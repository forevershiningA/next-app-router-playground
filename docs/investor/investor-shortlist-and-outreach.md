# Forever Shining — investor shortlist and outreach plan

**Prepared:** 16 September 2026  
**Recommendation:** run a dual-track process: (1) a small pre-seed syndicate to fund validation, and (2) paid pilot conversations with strategic operators. At the present evidence level, strategic pilots are more valuable than trying to force a large institutional VC round.

## Positioning for every conversation

> Forever Shining is an established, revenue-generating Australian memorial-commerce business with 20 years of operating experience. Its existing configurator has operated for approximately seven years. The company is now building a next-generation 3D, catalogue-aware studio to modernise the sales workflow and, once proven, offer it to stonemasons, funeral homes and memorial parks.

Do not lead with "an online headstone shop." It shrinks the outcome to retail and obscures the repeatable software/distribution opportunity. Equally, do not call it SaaS until a paid partner is using it; say **"a hybrid commerce and fulfilment platform, with B2B2C software being validated."**

### Deliberate exclusions

- **Tractor Ventures** is a good non-dilutive option only after the business reaches meaningful recurring revenue/GMV (its site states a $50k+ monthly-revenue threshold for several products). It is not a pre-revenue pre-seed substitute.
- Large generalist funds should be approached after proof of repeatable sales or paid B2B pilots. Cold outreach now will usually be too early and can waste a valuable first impression.
- A strategic operator can be a customer, distribution partner, or acquirer as well as an investor. Keep the pilot commercial; do not grant exclusivity or broad IP rights in exchange for an exploratory meeting.

## Recommended sequence — 30 days

1. **Week 1 — investment readiness.** Fill all deck placeholders; prepare a one-page financial model, cap table, revenue/order funnel, current cash/runway, data room and a 90-second live demo.
2. **Week 2 — warm introductions.** Seek one warm introduction each to Black Sheep and Common Sense. Send a concise note and a link to the deck, never an attachment on first contact if an investor has a submission form.
3. **Weeks 2–3 — 10 discovery calls.** Five independent stonemasons/memorial suppliers and the two strategic operators above. The goal is a paid pilot LOI, not a vague endorsement.
4. **Week 3 — syndicate.** Once one investor is engaged, invite Davis and relevant angels to fill the round. Record every question and objection.
5. **Week 4 — institutional follow-up.** Take the pilot pipeline, conversion funnel and updated deck to Rampersand/Investible.

## Before sending: non-negotiable diligence gaps

Investors will ask these in the first meeting. Prepare evidence, not narrative.

- Current revenue, orders, average order value, gross margin and refund/cancellation rate.
- A deduplicated, consent-safe historical design count. The public generator indexes expose 3,021 Forever Shining AU records dated 2019–2024 and 1,100 HeadstonesDesigner records dated 2023–2024. Treat this as an audit lead, not customer/order traction: records can include samples, drafts or duplicates.
- Funnel by channel: visitors → design starts → saves → quotes → orders; monthly cohort trend.
- CAC, sales cycle, time spent per proof/order and contribution margin.
- Founder names, ownership, full-time commitment, incorporation/jurisdiction and cap table.
- IP rights to software, 3D assets, catalogue data, customer images and brand; privacy and consent handling.
- Public-generator privacy review: the indexes contain design metadata, including design names. Before investor sharing or growth activity, verify that no personal memorial data is unintentionally indexable and document consent/retention/access controls.
- Payment, production and fulfilment responsibility. Be explicit about merchant of record by product category, partner economics, installer liability, delivery promise and whether Forever Shining is principal, agent, software vendor or a blend.
- Security and payments readiness. The internal audit notes outstanding work such as rate limiting and final production/payment deployment validation; fix and document these before scale claims.

## Success metrics for an investor process

Within 90 days, target a decision-quality package:

| Metric | Minimum decision signal |
|---|---|
| Partner interest | 3 signed paid pilots or LOIs with named owners and timeline |
| Sales evidence | Baseline funnel and at least two months of comparable conversion data |
| Unit economics | Channel-level CAC and contribution-margin hypothesis tested with real spend/orders |
| Fundraising | One credible anchor conversation and a clean data room |
| Product | Partner catalogue onboarding and proof-to-production workflow demonstrated end-to-end |

The exact numerical targets should be set from actual order economics. Do not manufacture ARR, TAM, retention or customer figures to make the deck look venture-ready.
