# Standard memorial size presets

The Select Size panel uses the selected display currency to choose a small set
of representative catalogue dimensions. All pairs are **width × vertical
height**, in whole millimetres internally. USD displays inches; GBP, EUR, AUD
and CAD retain the designer's existing millimetre display.

Headstone presets set the tablet width and height, and automatically fit its
visible base in all three dimensions. The tablet thickness remains unchanged.
The base uses the smallest valid regional preset with at least 20% extra width,
or the widest available preset when that overhang is not possible. If none fit,
dimensions are generated within the product limits. Base depth is 30% of its
width or tablet depth + 100 mm, whichever is larger, clamped to catalogue limits.
These proportions are a design default rather than additional regional standards.
Selecting a smaller headstone also reduces an oversized base. No Base remains
hidden. Base presets still set only base width and height. A currency
change updates the choices without resizing the design. Manually editing the
dimensions automatically clears the highlighted preset unless they match again.

Options outside the current product's dimension limits are disabled. Base widths
must also be at least the current headstone width. Existing store setters retain
the normal synchronization of bases, ledgers and kerbs.

These are representative starting sizes, not a country-wide or Europe-wide
cemetery standard. EUR uses a small metric selection from a German catalogue;
Canada uses Canadian and North American base sizes. Local cemetery requirements
still determine the final installed dimensions.

## Reference catalogues

- USD: [Cache Valley Monuments](https://cachevalleymonuments.com/most-popular-types-of-headstones/)
  lists 18 × 24, 24 × 18, 36 × 24 and 42 × 24 inch tablet faces and
  30 × 6 and 36 × 6 inch base faces.
  [Sierra Headstone](https://www.sierraheadstone.com/guides/headstone-sizes)
  lists the 48 × 6 inch base face.
  [Grassmarkers](https://www.grassmarkers.com/upright_memorial.php)
  supplies the 24 × 24 inch tablet example.
  [Twin City Monument](https://twincitymonument.com/memorial-options/memorial-styles/)
  lists 32 × 6 and 40 × 6 inch base faces.
- GBP: [J. Gumbrill](https://jgumbrill.co.uk/memorial-shape-and-sizes/)
  lists 17 × 24, 21 × 27 and 24 × 30 inch tablet faces. Rounded whole-mm pairs:
  432 × 610, 533 × 686 and 610 × 762.
  Base faces: 600 × 100 mm from [Peaceyard Ada](https://peaceyard.com/models/ada),
  750 × 100 mm from [R. French Stonemasonry](https://rfrenchstonemasonry.co.uk/staging/3154/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Headstone-Price-list.pdf),
  and 600 × 150 mm from [Robertson Granite Wells](https://robertsongranite.co.uk/products/lawn-memorials/wells).
- EUR: [Granitt / Grabmale Heidebrecht](https://www.granitt.de/angebote/)
  supplies the portrait 500 × 800 mm, 800 × 900 mm, landscape 1000 × 800 mm
  and square 1000 × 1000 mm tablet examples, and 600 × 150, 1000 × 150
  and 1200 × 190 mm base examples. Catalogue dimensions are normalized to
  width × height here; base front-to-back depth is not included.
- AUD: [Edstein headstone range](https://edstein.com.au/downloads/Edstein_Headstone_Range.pdf)
  lists tablet faces 650 × 700, 750 × 460 and 950 × 500 mm, plus base faces
  950 × 100, 1000 × 100 and 1200 × 100 mm.
- CAD: [Casket Depot Vancouver](https://casketdepotvancouver.ca/products/upright-monument)
  lists tablet faces 30 × 24, 24 × 30, 26 × 36 and 36 × 24 inches, plus
  base faces 36 × 6 and 44 × 6 inches. The 32 × 6 inch base is also listed
  by [Twin City Monument](https://twincitymonument.com/memorial-options/memorial-styles/).

Sources reviewed 2026-10-26. Inch sizes are converted using 25.4 mm/in and rounded
to whole millimetres to match the store. Imperial display tolerates this rounding
so, for example, 610 mm remains labelled 24 inches rather than 25 inches.
